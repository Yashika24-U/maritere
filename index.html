You Have More Control Than You Think

The science behind lifestyle choices and disease prevention.

Current Reality What You Can Control
6 in 10 adults have a chronic disease Up to 80% of chronic diseases can be prevented through healthy eating, physical activity, avoiding tobacco, and maintaining a healthy weight
4 in 10 have multiple chronic diseases Healthy diet + movement + no smoking = ≈78% lower risk of multiple illnesses
33% of deaths are from heart disease & stroke Risk lowered by up to 80% with diet, activity, weight management, no smoking, low alcohol intake
1.7M new cancer diagnoses yearly At least 40% of cancers are preventable through lifestyle habits
Alzheimer’s is the 6th leading cause of death 40% of dementia cases may be preventable/delayed via exercise, diet, blood pressure/diabetes control
37 million Americans have diabetes Type 2 diabetes risk can be reduced by up to 90% through lifestyle habits
1 in 7 adults has chronic kidney disease (CKD) Healthy lifestyle habits can reduce CKD risk by ≈68%
42% of U.S. adults live with obesity Nutrition+activity+behavioral support can prevent obesity and reduce risks by 50–80%
