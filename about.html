About Dr. Maritere Rochet

Physician-Guided, Evidence-Based Wellness Care for Lasting Health

Meet Your Doctor

Dedicated to Your Whole-Person Wellness

Dr. Maritere Rochet, MD, MPH, DipABLM leads with a physician-guided, evidence-based approach to wellness care. As a Lifestyle Medicine physician, she conducts a comprehensive assessment of six key pillars of health — nutrition, physical activity, restorative sleep, stress management, healthy relationships, and avoidance of risky substances.

Based on these insights, she designs personalized care plans to prevent, improve, and even reverse chronic disease. Working collaboratively with primary care physicians and specialists, she ensures a holistic, coordinated approach to lasting healing.

As a military spouse and practicing physician, she has served diverse communities across the U.S., offering culturally sensitive, personalized care. Fluent in both English and Spanish, Dr. Rochet ensures every patient feels seen, supported, and understood.

Our Foundation

Approach & Philosophy

My Approach

Dr. Rochet's approach centers on identifying and addressing the root causes of illness while supporting the body's natural ability to restore balance and thrive. This personalized, supportive path helps patients heal from within.

My Philosophy

True health is not simply the absence of disease—it is the presence of energy, joy, and a deep connection to one's authentic self. Care is grounded in science, delivered with compassion, and built on partnership and trust.

Patient Partnership

Every patient journey is unique. Dr. Rochet works collaboratively with you and your healthcare team to create sustainable lifestyle changes that enhance energy, vitality, and overall well-being.
