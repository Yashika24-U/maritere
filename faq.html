These answers reflect the most common questions from patients interested in a science-based, physician-guided approach to wellness, prevention, and sustainable healing.

What is Lifestyle Medicine?

Lifestyle Medicine is a board-certified medical specialty that uses therapeutic lifestyle interventions — such as nutrition, physical activity, restorative sleep, stress management, social connection, and avoidance of risky substances — to prevent, treat, and often reverse chronic disease. It is an evidence-based, physician-guided discipline focused on addressing the root causes of illness rather than managing symptoms alone. In short: Lifestyle Medicine is not an alternative to traditional medicine — it’s the foundation of it.

How is Lifestyle Medicine different from functional or alternative medicine?

Lifestyle Medicine is a board-certified, evidence-based medical specialty that uses proven lifestyle interventions — including nutrition, movement, sleep, stress management, social connection, and avoidance of risky substances — as the primary treatment for chronic disease.
While functional and alternative medicine may incorporate complementary or supplemental therapies, Lifestyle Medicine is distinguished by its scientific rigor, standardized medical training, and clinical guidelines.
It integrates seamlessly with traditional medical care to prevent, treat, and often reverse disease by addressing its root causes through sustainable, evidence-based behavior change.

Do you accept insurance?

The Living Practice MD is a cash-based Lifestyle Medicine practice, allowing for longer, more personalized visits and a focus on prevention rather than symptom management.
Payment is due at the time of service, and superbills can be provided so that patients may request potential reimbursement from their insurance company, depending on their plan’s benefits.

Do you replace my primary care physician?

No. Dr. Rochet works collaboratively with your primary care doctor and specialists to ensure continuity and safety.
Her goal is to bridge the gap between traditional care and preventive health — offering Lifestyle Medicine support that complements your existing medical team.

What conditions can benefit from Lifestyle Medicine?

Lifestyle Medicine is especially effective for:

  • Type 2 Diabetes
  • Heart disease and hypertension
  • High cholesterol
  • Obesity and metabolic syndrome
  • Fatty liver disease
  • PCOS and hormonal imbalance
  • Anxiety, depression, and insomnia
  • Early cognitive decline
  • Osteoarthritis and inflammatory conditions
  • Cancer prevention and survivorship
By addressing the root causes of disease, Lifestyle Medicine helps prevent, improve, and often reverse chronic conditions.

How long are visits and how often will I see you?

Initial visits are typically 60 minutes, allowing time to explore your history, goals, and lifestyle factors.
Follow-up frequency depends on your personalized plan but often ranges from monthly to quarterly, with progress assessments and refinements as you move toward your goals.

Can you help me if I’m already on medication?

Absolutely. Many patients experience measurable improvements that allow for safe medication reduction under medical supervision.
Lifestyle Medicine enhances — rather than conflicts with — pharmacologic treatment by supporting the body’s natural ability to heal and rebalance.

What can I expect during my first visit?

Your first consultation includes a comprehensive Lifestyle Medicine assessment across the six pillars of health, a review of labs and medical history, and a personalized action plan.
You’ll leave with clear next steps and practical tools to begin implementing change right away.

Do you offer telehealth appointments?

Yes. Virtual visits are available for established patients throughout Florida, providing convenience and flexibility without compromising the quality or continuity of care.

Can I work with you if I’m not sick but want to feel better or age well?

Yes. Many patients choose Lifestyle Medicine for prevention, longevity, and optimization — not only for disease management.
Together, we’ll design a personalized plan to enhance your vitality, energy, and emotional well-being at every stage of life.

What makes Lifestyle Medicine different from health coaching or nutrition counseling?

Lifestyle Medicine is guided by board-certified physicians trained to evaluate medical history, interpret labs, and identify the biological, psychological, social, and environmental factors that influence health.
It focuses on clinical prevention and disease reversal using evidence-based interventions, often in collaboration with health coaches, dietitians, and other allied professionals as part of a team approach.

Do you order labs or review existing lab results?

Yes. Dr. Rochet reviews your existing lab results and may recommend additional tests when clinically appropriate.
Lab findings are interpreted through a Lifestyle Medicine lens — focusing on prevention, root causes, and measurable improvements over time.

Can Lifestyle Medicine really reverse disease?

Yes — in many cases. Lifestyle Medicine interventions can reverse the progression of conditions such as type 2 diabetes, hypertension, obesity, fatty liver disease, and early heart disease.
Reversal occurs when key health markers — like blood sugar, blood pressure, or cholesterol — return to healthy ranges and the body’s underlying biology begins to heal.
These outcomes are supported by decades of clinical research showing that sustainable lifestyle changes can restore health and prevent disease progression.

What happens after I finish a program or initial visits?

After completing your initial plan or program, you can continue with follow-up visits to maintain progress, adjust goals, and stay accountable.
Many patients transition into a maintenance or longevity plan for continued support in optimizing health and preventing relapse.

Can you work with my other specialists (like a cardiologist or endocrinologist)?

Yes. Collaboration is central to Dr. Rochet’s approach. With your consent, she communicates with your other healthcare providers to ensure your Lifestyle Medicine plan complements and enhances your overall treatment strategy.

Do you recommend supplements or medications?

Lifestyle Medicine focuses on evidence-based lifestyle interventions as the foundation of care.
However, when clinically appropriate, Dr. Rochet may recommend medications or supplements supported by strong scientific evidence to complement your individualized plan and ensure safe, effective outcomes.

How is Lifestyle Medicine different from concierge or primary care?

Unlike concierge or primary care models, Lifestyle Medicine focuses exclusively on preventive, root-cause, and behavior-based interventions rather than routine medical management or urgent care.
It’s designed for patients who want to optimize long-term health and longevity through scientifically grounded lifestyle transformation.
