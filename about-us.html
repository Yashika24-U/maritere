Meet Dr. Maritere Rochet, MD, MPH, DipABLM

Physician-Guided, Evidence-Based Wellness Care As a Lifestyle Medicine physician, I conduct a comprehensive assessment of your six pillars of health — nutrition, physical activity, restorative sleep, stress management, healthy relationships, and avoidance of risky substances — and design a personalized plan to help you prevent, improve, and even reverse chronic disease. I work collaboratively with your primary care physician and specialists to complement your care, bridge gaps, and support meaningful, lasting healing. My approach focuses on identifying and addressing the root causes of illness while supporting your body’s natural ability to restore balance and thrive. Together, we create a personalized, supportive path that fosters healing from within, prevents future disease, and enhances your energy, vitality, and overall well-being. What sets me apart is delivering a physician-guided, evidence-based wellness program through the lens of a medical doctor — empowering you to live longer, feel more vibrant, and thrive fully. My Philosophy I believe true health is not simply the absence of disease — it is the presence of energy, joy, and a deep connection to your most authentic self. My care is grounded in science, delivered with compassion, and built on a foundation of partnership and trust. Credentials & Background Dr. Maritere Rochet is a double board-certified Family Medicine and Lifestyle Medicine physician with over 15 years of experience in primary care and whole-person wellness. She also holds a Master of Public Health, with a strong emphasis on prevention and empowering patients to take an active role in their health. As a military spouse and practicing physician, Dr. Rochet has cared for diverse communities across multiple U.S. states, offering culturally sensitive, personalized care.
