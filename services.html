Transform Your Health with Lifestyle Medicine

Lifestyle Medicine is a growing medical specialty, formally recognized for nearly two decades, with board certification available since 2017 through the American Board of Lifestyle Medicine. There are currently around 3,000 board-certified Lifestyle Medicine physicians in the U.S. — and Dr. Rochet is proud to be one of them.

Lifestyle Medicine is especially effective for conditions such as type 2 diabetes, heart disease, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, obesity, metabolic syndrome, fatty liver disease, and early cognitive decline. It also supports healing in conditions like polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), certain autoimmune diseases, osteoarthritis, sleep disorders, gastrointestinal issues (such as IBS), mild to moderate anxiety and depression, and cancer prevention and survivorship.

Therapeutic lifestyle interventions guided by a board-certified Lifestyle Medicine physician can benefit nearly anyone — whether you’re hoping to:

  • Prevent disease
  • Reduce your risk due to family history
  • Improve or even reverse an existing chronic condition
  • Complement your current medical care
  • Or simply optimize your long-term health and well-being

Real Results with Lifestyle Medicine

  • Fewer Medications, Fewer Side Effects

    • Lifestyle interventions can reduce or even eliminate the need for certain medications, helping patients avoid side effects and drug interactions.

  • Better Outcomes Than Conventional Care Alone

    • In many chronic conditions, Lifestyle Medicine has been shown to be as effective — or more effective — than standard treatments, slowing or even reversing disease progression.

  • Safe, Sustainable Improvements in Key Health Markers

    • Nutrition, movement, sleep, stress reduction, and other lifestyle changes can lead to lasting improvements in blood pressure, cholesterol, blood sugar, inflammation, and weight — often with fewer risks than medications alone.
